# Impact of AI on Software Engineering and Hiring

## Overview

Analysis of how AI tools are transforming developer productivity (5x-10x increase), leading to changes in hiring plans (less aggressive engineering hiring) and interview processes (assessing candidates' ability to leverage AI tools). Also, how AI blurs the lines between traditional and 'vibe' coders and improves support quality.
