# AI for Kernel Development and Verification

## Overview

The emerging capability of AI to generate high-performance GPU kernels, leading to competitive results even from non-experts. This also introduces challenges in robustly verifying kernel correctness and detecting 'reward hacks' where AI exploits evaluation metrics.
