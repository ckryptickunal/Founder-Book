# The First Dedicated YC GPU Cluster - With Together AI

## Metadata

- Video ID: `tOhkv307Pm0`

## Summary

YC and Together AI have partnered to launch the first dedicated YC GPU cluster, providing AI-native startups within the YC portfolio with easier, more flexible, and cost-effective access to crucial compute resources. This initiative directly addresses the significant industry-wide bottleneck of GPU capacity and high costs, enabling startups to scale efficiently, make shorter-term compute commitments, and benefit from Together AI's expertise in AI systems research and optimized infrastructure. The partnership aims to empower founders by providing shared resources, akin to a 'founders union,' to level the playing field against larger companies.

## Key Ideas

- YC and Together AI have partnered to create the first dedicated YC GPU cluster for AI-native startups.
- The partnership aims to solve the critical bottleneck of GPU capacity and high compute costs for early-stage AI companies.
- Together AI offers a comprehensive cloud service covering the entire generative AI lifecycle, from model building to serving at scale.
- AI-native startups have diverse and heterogeneous compute needs, ranging from small research groups to large-scale model training and inference.
- YC positions itself as a 'founders union,' providing shared resources like the GPU cluster to give startups leverage typically held by larger companies.
- Together AI's deep research in AI systems (e.g., Flash Attention, Mamba architecture) directly translates into product benefits, offering optimized workloads and better unit economics for customers.
- The partnership allows startups to make shorter-term compute commitments (weeks/months instead of years), preventing them from locking up significant capital upfront.
- Compute planning is a complex and often the largest expense for AI development, requiring strategic financial management for startups.

## Notable Claims

- Generative AI was going to be a very foundational technology. Evidence: This was the core conviction of Together AI's co-founders when they started the company four years ago, leading to their focus on building a platform for it.
- Access to capacity [for GPUs] is a really big problem, let alone great pricing. Evidence: The speaker contrasts the current difficulty with 8 years ago when AWS instances were readily available. This bottleneck is the primary motivation for the YC-Together AI partnership.
