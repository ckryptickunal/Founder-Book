# What if Jung was your private diary writer? Rosebud AI journaling as therapy is here

## Summary

Garry Tan interviews Chris, co-founder of Rosebud AI, an interactive journaling app designed with therapists to foster self-awareness and positive change. Chris explains how Rosebud leverages AI to provide empathic listening, ask probing follow-up questions, and maintain long-term memory, addressing the challenges of traditional journaling and the reluctance some people have to share with others. They discuss Rosebud's unique AI design, which prioritizes precise, non-solution-oriented responses, and its integration of therapeutic modalities like IFS, ACT, and CBT. The conversation also explores the evolving role of AI in mental health support, the importance of designing AI-human relationships with boundaries, and the economic challenges of consumer AI products, emphasizing smart context management over simply larger models. Chris reflects on his career's consistent theme of helping people connect more meaningfully with themselves and others.

## Key Ideas

- Rosebud AI is an interactive journal that uses AI, designed with therapists, to promote self-awareness and positive change by making journaling more rewarding and accessible.
- AI can function as an infinitely patient and empathic listener, providing support without immediately offering solutions, similar to a good coach or therapist.
- The ability of AI to maintain long-term memory and personalize interactions is a crucial differentiator, addressing a fundamental human need for being remembered and cared for.
- Rosebud AI aims to provide a foundational layer of mental health support, complementing human therapy and potentially serving as an on-ramp to professional help.
- Effective AI design for therapeutic applications requires careful consideration of boundaries in AI-human relationships to prevent unhealthy dependencies.
- Integrating established therapeutic modalities (like IFS, ACT, CBT) enhances the efficacy and depth of AI-driven journaling experiences.
- The unit economics of consumer AI products necessitate innovative approaches to cost management and context handling, as simply relying on larger, more expensive models is not sustainable.
- The future of software development with AI involves generative UI and dynamic, hyper-personalized user experiences that adapt to individual needs and contexts.

## Notable Claims

- Journaling has been scientifically proven to be good for mental health. Evidence: Chris states: 'journaling has been scientifically proven to be uh good for us and and healthy for our mental health'.
- Large Language Models (LLMs) are proving to be smart, infinitely patient, and infinitely empathic. Evidence: Garry Tan states: 'llms are turning out to be really smart and sort of infinitely patient infinitely empathic'.
- A good therapist believes the client already possesses all the answers within themselves. Evidence: Chris states: 'A good therapist will tell you that you already have all the answers when you walk in the door'.
- Many people are afraid to talk to friends, loved ones, or professionals about their problems due to fear of burdening others. Evidence: Chris states: 'how many people are actually afraid to to talk to their friends or to loved ones or even to professionals... people actually don't want to uh create a burden on others'.
- Rosebud's prompt engineering and data integration provide a competitive advantage and intellectual property (IP) over larger AI players. Evidence: Chris states: 'there's actually a competitive advantage and there's actually IP in the prompt engineering and developing these different experiences and once you pull in all the the data and the memory and everything that you could pull in to make the experience even more personalized that's where you really start to to get things to be hyper personalized and interesting in a way that you're not going to get from the bigger players right now'.
- AI has opened up entirely new markets in consumer software that were previously considered 'tarpits'. Evidence: Garry Tan states: 'AI has suddenly just opened up all of these brand new markets like the markets that frankly previously we might have considered tarpits are not tarpits anymore'.
- AI's strength lies in tailoring experiences and generating dynamic UI based on live context. Evidence: Chris states: 'the thing that AI does really well is that it can tailor uh it can take live context and generate something based off of that... we're actually able to generate UI elements from the AI'.
- Rosebud AI will never replace human therapy. Evidence: Chris states: 'it's never going to replace therapy nor should it'.
- ChatGPT's current method of handling memory (generating facts on the fly) is 'wrong' compared to Rosebud's 'context at read' approach. Evidence: Chris states: 'the way that like chat gp2 does memory now I think is just wrong it generates facts like on the Fly and then saves those facts... whereas context at read which is what we do is a lot more effective'.
- Startups in consumer AI are incentivized to keep costs down and offer fixed pricing due to competition from giants and customer expectations. Evidence: Chris states: 'startups like us are incentivized to try to keep costs down and figure out how to deliver the best results... we decided instead to just kind of off escate all of that and set a fixed price... that allows us to ensure that we're seeing some profit margin on every user'.
