# Why You're Getting Zero Replies To Your Cold Emails

## Metadata

## Summary

A Y Combinator visiting partner provides a strategic guide for founders to improve cold email and outbound sales performance, emphasizing manual outreach, precise targeting, and customer-centric messaging over automation.

## Key Ideas

- Start with manual, personalized outreach before using automation tools to ensure you learn what works.
- Targeting the right person with the right pain point is more important than perfect email copy.
- Use intent signals like job postings and company growth stages to identify prospects who need your product.
- Keep emails short (5-8 sentences) and focus on solving the buyer's problems rather than listing product features.
- Optimize your LinkedIn profile to act as a landing page that builds trust and credibility.
- Treat outbound as a disciplined, scheduled habit rather than an occasional task.
- Use the exact language and feedback from existing customers to refine your messaging.

## Notable Claims

- Automating outreach too early prevents founders from learning why their messaging is failing. Evidence: If you blast 1,000 emails without manual testing, you gain no data on whether the failure is due to targeting, messaging, or deliverability.
- The number of mutual connections is the biggest predictor of whether a LinkedIn connection request is accepted. Evidence: The speaker identifies this as the primary factor in building trust and acceptance rates on the platform.
- Breakup emails have higher reply rates than standard follow-ups. Evidence: They provide a low-pressure environment for the prospect to say 'no,' which is still a valuable data point for the founder.
