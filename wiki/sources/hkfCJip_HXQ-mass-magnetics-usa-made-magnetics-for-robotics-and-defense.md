# Mass Magnetics: USA-made magnetics for robotics and defense

## Summary

Mass Magnetics, founded by Eric Silver and Matt, specializes in recycling rare earth magnets from electric vehicle (EV) motors to supply the robotics and defense industries. By bypassing traditional, capital-intensive mining and refining processes, the company uses a proprietary sintering process to create high-quality magnets domestically, aiming to solve critical supply chain dependencies.

## Key Ideas

- Recycling rare earth magnets from EV motors is more capital-efficient than domestic mining.
- The company uses a specialized sintering process to convert recycled magnetic material into new magnets.
- Rare earth magnets (neodymium, dysprosium) are critical for both defense applications and the future of robotics.
- Hardware recursion—where robots build robots—will necessitate a circular economy for magnetic materials.
- The company aims to eliminate the refining bottleneck in magnetic metallurgy.

- [[topics/magnetic-metallurgy|Magnetic Metallurgy]]: The science of processing magnetic powders through sintering to create functional components for motors.
- [[topics/supply-chain-security|Supply Chain Security]]: Reducing dependence on foreign mining and refining for critical minerals like neodymium and dysprosium.

## Notable Claims

- 93% of rare earth magnetic material is currently thrown away. Evidence: Stated by co-founder Eric Silver based on industry material flow analysis.
- Mass Magnetics' process is significantly more capital-light than traditional mining. Evidence: By skipping the refining stage and circularizing existing material, the company avoids the massive infrastructure costs of mining.
