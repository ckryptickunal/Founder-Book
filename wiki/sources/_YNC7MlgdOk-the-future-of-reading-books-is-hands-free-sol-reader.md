# The future of reading books is hands free | SOL Reader

## Summary

The video introduces the SOL Reader, the world's first wearable e-reader, developed by Ben. It aims to provide a comfortable, distraction-free, and fully immersive reading experience, allowing users to read long-form content without the physical discomfort of holding a book or the digital distractions of modern devices. The device is designed for various environments, from bed to airplanes, and focuses on enhancing "actual reality" through "face tech" rather than complex AR/VR. The creators envision it as a tool to restore deep focus and creativity, moving personal computing back towards its original ideal of being a "bicycle for the mind."

## Key Ideas

- The SOL Reader is the world's first wearable e-reader, offering a hands-free, immersive reading experience.
- It is designed to eliminate distractions and physical discomfort associated with traditional reading, promoting deeper engagement with books.
- The device focuses on a single use case (reading) to achieve a lightweight design, long battery life, and optimal user experience, contrasting with the complexities of general-purpose AR/VR.
- The user experience is crafted to evoke anticipation, deep immersion, and a refreshed, edified feeling after reading.
- The broader vision is to combat modern digital distractions and re-enable deep focus and creativity in humanity, aligning with the original ideal of personal computing.
