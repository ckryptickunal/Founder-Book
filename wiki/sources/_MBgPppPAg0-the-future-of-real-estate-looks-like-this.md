# The Future of Real Estate looks like THIS

## Summary

This video features Garry Tan interviewing Brendan Wallace, co-founder of Fifth Wall, the largest real estate venture firm. They discuss the massive, yet largely undigitized, real estate industry (PropTech) and how technology is transforming it. The conversation highlights Fifth Wall's unique investment strategy, the broken aspects of home buying, and successful PropTech companies like OpenDoor, Hippo, Doma, and Blend. They conclude by identifying commercial real estate as the next major frontier for technological innovation, mirroring the advancements seen in the residential sector.

## Key Ideas

- Real estate is a trillion-dollar industry (13% of global economy) that is significantly undigitized and ripe for technological disruption.
- PropTech (real estate technology) is a rapidly growing sector, attracting substantial investment and IPOs.
- Fifth Wall's strategic advantage comes from its limited partners being major real estate firms, providing end-users and partners for their portfolio companies.
- The home buying process is 'anachronistic' and 'archaic,' characterized by a lack of information and reliance on antiquated offline processes.
- Technology is streamlining various aspects of residential real estate, including iBuying (OpenDoor), digital home insurance (Hippo), digital title insurance (Doma), and digital mortgage origination (Blend).
- The next significant wave of innovation in PropTech is expected in commercial real estate, applying insights and technologies developed for the consumer/residential side to complex commercial transactions.
