# Introducing Early Access: An Invite only program for the Enterprise

## Metadata

- Video ID: `2JiUeYzcRKE`
- Channel: Y Combinator
- Published: 2026-09-08T16:13:54Z
- URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2JiUeYzcRKE

## Summary

Y Combinator introduces the 'YC Early Access Network', an invite-only program designed to connect enterprise executives with handpicked, high-potential AI startups during their YC batch to streamline AI adoption.

## Key Ideas

- Enterprise companies face difficulty navigating the crowded AI market between aggressive legacy vendors and thousands of unproven startups.
- The YC Early Access Network curates the best AI startups for enterprise leaders to evaluate.
- The program facilitates direct feedback loops between enterprise executives and early-stage founders.
- Participants gain access to a peer network of executives facing similar AI integration challenges.

## Entities

- [[entities/y-combinator|Y Combinator]] (organization): A startup accelerator that provides seed funding and mentorship.
- [[entities/yc-early-access-network|YC Early Access Network]] (project): An invite-only program connecting enterprise executives with YC-backed AI startups.

## Topics

- [[topics/enterprise-ai-adoption|Enterprise AI Adoption]]: The challenges large organizations face in identifying and implementing effective AI solutions.
- [[topics/startup-enterprise-collaboration|Startup-Enterprise Collaboration]]: Creating structured environments for early-stage founders to receive feedback from potential enterprise customers.

## Notable Claims

- Enterprise CIOs are currently overwhelmed by the sheer volume of AI startups and underwhelmed by legacy vendor AI offerings. Evidence: Anecdotal evidence from conversations with CIOs cited by the speaker.
- Engaging with startups during their YC batch provides a unique advantage for enterprise leaders. Evidence: The program allows leaders to explore 'what's happening at the edge' before these companies reach the broader market.

## Quotes

> There's a lot of noise for enterprise companies evaluating AI today.
> Being early is actually the point here.
> You'll join a peer group of top execs from public and private companies who are all evaluating and building AI.
