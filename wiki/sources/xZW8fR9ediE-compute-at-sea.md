## Summary

The transcript addresses the critical shortage of compute for AI, exacerbated by land-based data centers running out of electricity and land, facing lengthy approvals, and local opposition. It proposes an innovative solution: moving compute offshore to 'compute flotillas' – standardized modular vessels operating as a global cloud. The ocean offers vast surface area, abundant sunlight, no permitting process, and acts as a natural heat sink. Y Combinator is actively seeking to fund founders working on this offshore computing concept.

## Key Ideas

- Artificial intelligence is facing a severe compute shortage.
- Traditional land-based data centers are constrained by limited electricity, land availability, long approval processes, and local government opposition.
- Moving compute offshore to the ocean is proposed as a viable solution to these challenges.
- The ocean provides significant advantages for computing infrastructure, including vast space (70% of Earth's surface), abundant sunlight, absence of land-based permitting, and a natural heat sink.
- The proposed solution involves 'compute flotillas,' which are standardized, modular vessels operating collectively as a global cloud.
- Y Combinator is actively looking to fund startups and founders who are developing solutions for offshore computing.
