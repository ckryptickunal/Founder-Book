# How Outset Turned AI Interviews Into a New Category

## Summary

Aaron Cannon, CEO of Outset, discusses how his company pioneered AI-led customer research, evolving from a conversational survey tool into a comprehensive platform that uses AI to conduct deep, human-like interviews and generate digital twins for predictive consumer behavior analysis.

## Key Ideas

- AI-led interviews provide the scale of surveys with the depth of qualitative research.
- Early-stage startups often face resistance when introducing new categories because they lack existing budget line items.
- Market education and case studies are essential for overcoming skepticism in enterprise sales.
- AI can elicit more honest, 'ground truth' data from users than human interviewers due to the lack of social judgment.
- Digital twins allow companies to simulate customer feedback and predict behavior without the time and cost of traditional research.
- The evolution of LLMs has enabled Outset to move from simple text-based surveys to multi-modal interviews involving visual intelligence and co-design.

## Notable Claims

- People share more honest information with AI than with human interviewers. Evidence: Users feel less social judgment and pressure to perform when interacting with an AI, leading to 'ground truth' data.
- Digital twins can effectively predict human behavior for business decision-making. Evidence: By grounding AI models in extensive, hour-long interviews with real people, the resulting twins can simulate how those individuals would react to new products or pricing.
