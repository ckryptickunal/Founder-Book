# He Built the World's #1 Open-Source Coding Agent

## Metadata

- Video ID: `_O6x4ktK6JA`
- Channel: Y Combinator
- Published: 2026-07-24T14:00:17Z

## Summary

Jay V, founder and CEO of Open Code, discusses the explosive global growth of his open-source coding agent, which serves as an alternative to proprietary solutions like Claude Code. Open Code has achieved 13 million monthly active users and up to $40 million in annualized revenue by offering model choice, affordability, and a superior developer experience. The company's growth was significantly boosted by an incident where Anthropic inadvertently elevated Open Code's profile. Jay V also shares his 16-year entrepreneurial journey, highlighting persistence through multiple Y Combinator applications and product iterations, culminating in Open Code's success by addressing a broad market from individual developers to Fortune 500 enterprises.

## Key Ideas

- The market for coding agents and AI intelligence is unprecedented and rapidly expanding globally.
- Open Code's rapid growth (20x increase in users, 7 trillion tokens/day) is driven by its open-source nature, affordability, and support for diverse models.
- A strategic incident with Anthropic, where they attempted to block Open Code, inadvertently served as a major inflection point for Open Code's visibility and user acquisition.
- The gap between open-source and frontier LLMs is shrinking, making open-source models increasingly viable and preferred for real-world work due to cost and performance.
- Open Code possesses unique global usage data, revealing insights into model popularity (e.g., DeepSeek Flash, GLM) and user behavior, especially in developing countries and among cost-conscious users.
- AI token economics involve subsidizing initial usage (CAC via tokens) to attract 'whales' (large enterprise customers) who drive profitability.
- Open Code operates as a 'marketplace' for LLMs, fostering competition among model labs, which ultimately benefits consumers through better choice and pricing.
- The founder's 16-year journey of persistence, learning from past ventures (including building terminal UIs and a serverless platform), and 'building in public' laid the groundwork for Open Code's 'overnight success'.
- Intentional product design choices, such as the name 'OpenCode' and immediate support for 70+ models, were crucial for establishing Open Code as the default open alternative.

## Notable Claims

- Most people in the world still haven't experienced the magic of a coding agent. Evidence: Stated by the speaker as the premise for Open Code's mission to make coding agents accessible globally.
- The market for intelligence has not existed before. Evidence: Stated by a speaker, emphasizing the unprecedented nature of the current AI market.
- Open Code has been growing at an astounding rate this year. Evidence: Reached 4.6 million weekly active users, 13 million monthly active users (20x increase since year start), processes 7 trillion tokens per day, and has an annualized revenue of $38-40 million from inference and $18 million from subscriptions.
- 5% of all Codex subscribers choose Open Code as their main harness. Evidence: A tweet from Tibo, a lead engineer at Codex, cited by the host.
- Anthropic tried to clamp down on people using Open Code with Claude's code subscriptions. Evidence: Anthropic blocked requests if the system prompt mentioned 'open code'.
- The Anthropic incident inadvertently fueled Open Code's growth and equated it with Claude Code. Evidence: Users took notice, and many new users started looking into Open Code because of the ban, putting it on the same 'pedestal' as Claude Code.
- The gap between open-source models and frontier models has significantly shrunk, making open-weight models viable for real work. Evidence: Initially, open-source models were 6 months behind, but by August-September of the previous year, they started becoming good enough, triggering a wave of users to Open Code.
- Gemini 2.5 (or 2.4) was the first time an open-source model's usage surpassed that of Anthropic models on Open Code. Evidence: For a 4-week span in February, Open Code observed users using Gemini significantly more than Anthropic's Sonnet plus Opus models combined.
- DeepSeek Flash is the most used model on Open Code by token volume. Evidence: Data published on opencode.ai/data shows DeepSeek Flash leading, followed by DeepSeek Pro and GLM.
- Open Code has meaningful usage in China, accounting for 17% of its traffic. Evidence: Stated by Jay V, noting that many Chinese users prefer models built in their country, which Open Code supports.
- Dozens of Fortune 500 companies are using Open Code. Evidence: Open Code receives DMs and emails from large companies asking to fill out security questionnaires because thousands of their employees are already using the product.
- Open Code is the largest customer for most open-source models in terms of token volume. Evidence: Stated by Jay V, highlighting their symbiotic relationship with open-source model labs.
- Open Code benefits from a stable 24-hour GPU utilization cycle due to its global user base. Evidence: The peaks and troughs of GPU utilization are smoothed out by users in different time zones, leading to efficient unit economics.
- The company (Anomaly/Open Code) was incorporated in 2010, making it a 16-year-old legal entity. Evidence: Stated by the host and confirmed by Jay V.
- Jay V applied to YC nine times from 2016-2021 and had four interviews before being accepted. Evidence: Data provided by the host from YC records.
