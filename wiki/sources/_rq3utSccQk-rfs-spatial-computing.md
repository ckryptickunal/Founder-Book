## Summary

The speaker, with a decade of experience in augmented reality, posits that spatial computing is the next major platform, defining it as a combination of AR, VR, and real-world understanding with digital content rendering. Recent hardware advancements, specifically the Apple Vision Pro and Meta Quest 3, are making this vision achievable. This opens up significant opportunities for founders to develop innovative applications beyond gaming, such as infinite screens, 3D content interaction, and immersive collaborative experiences. Y Combinator encourages founders excited about this frontier to apply.

## Key Ideas

- Spatial computing is identified as the next major computing platform.
- Spatial computing is defined as a combination of augmented reality, virtual reality, and the ability to understand the real world and render digital content.
- Recent hardware advancements, particularly the Apple Vision Pro and Meta Quest 3, are crucial enablers for the widespread adoption of spatial computing.
- There are broad potential applications for spatial computing beyond gaming, including infinite screens, 3D content interaction, and immersive collaboration.
- Y Combinator is actively seeking and encouraging founders to build companies in the spatial computing space.
