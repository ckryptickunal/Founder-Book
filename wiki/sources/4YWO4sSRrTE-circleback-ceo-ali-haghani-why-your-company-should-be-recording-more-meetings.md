# Circleback CEO Ali Haghani: Why Your Company Should Be Recording More Meetings

## Summary

Ali Haghani, CEO of CircleBack, discusses how his company uses AI to transform meeting transcripts into a 'company brain.' He shares his personal workflow, the integration of AI agents into daily operations, and his philosophy on why companies should default to recording all meetings to provide necessary context for future AI-driven workflows.

## Key Ideas

- Recording all meetings is essential for providing context to AI agents, which will increasingly drive company operations.
- AI agents should be used for operational tasks and coding, but humans must remain in the loop for critical decisions like sending emails or architectural design.
- Software engineering is shifting from manual coding to orchestrating AI agents, making product strategy and system composition more important than raw coding speed.
- Companies should implement rigorous evaluation (evals) systems to ensure AI-generated outputs, such as meeting notes, maintain high quality and avoid redundant language.
- A 'company brain' approach allows teams to query past conversations, track action items, and maintain institutional memory across departments.

## Notable Claims

- Recording all company meetings is becoming a necessity for effective AI integration. Evidence: AI agents require historical context from past conversations to perform tasks accurately and avoid working in isolation.
- The bottleneck in software development is shifting from coding speed to product decision-making. Evidence: As AI lowers the cost of writing code, the value lies in what is built and how systems are composed.
