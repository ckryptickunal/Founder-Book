# The Best Time to Build in Crypto

## Metadata

- Video ID: `M861XfrOR5s`

## Summary

Despite a current crypto bear market with low prices and declining narratives, Y Combinator expresses strong optimism, having invested in over 100 crypto startups and expecting this number to grow significantly. They believe crypto rails will become ubiquitous for all YC startups, often without their direct knowledge. This bullish outlook is driven by increasing regulatory clarity, widespread stablecoin adoption by major financial institutions, the transformative potential of tokenized stocks, and the inherent advantages of building during bear markets, which foster genuine projects over speculative ones. YC highlights various projects they've funded, focusing on infrastructure, payments, and new applications across different regions.

## Key Ideas

- Y Combinator is highly optimistic about crypto despite the current bear market, viewing it as the best time to build.
- Crypto rails are expected to become foundational for all YC startups, even those not explicitly 'crypto' companies.
- Key drivers for optimism include regulatory clarity, institutional adoption of stablecoins, and the rise of tokenized stocks.
- Bear markets are considered superior for building real projects, as they filter out speculative ventures and attract dedicated founders.
- Y Combinator is funding diverse crypto projects, from major players to infrastructure and regional payment solutions.
