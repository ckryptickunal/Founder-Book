# New Operating Systems for the Physical World

## Metadata

- Video ID: `V_gDce8hBrg`

## Summary

The video highlights a significant opportunity for new operating systems in the physical world, where 80% of the global workforce operates but is underserved by outdated software. It explains how AI is introducing three new worker types—AI agents, field robots, and humans using wearables—which current operating systems are not designed to manage. This creates a massive market for startups to build integrated systems that manage human and robot labor, offering a substantial data advantage by recording all work performed, which existing incumbents and frontier models will lack.

## Key Ideas

- 80% of the global workforce operates in the physical world, yet the software for these industries is over 20 years old.
- AI is transforming physical world work by introducing three new worker types: AI agents, field robots, and humans using wearables.
- Existing operating systems are not equipped to manage the collaboration and interaction between these three diverse worker types.
- There are significant startup opportunities in developing new operating systems that can route jobs, ensure safety, and measure reliability across AI agents, robots, and humans.
- The market for these new operating systems is substantially larger than current software solutions, as industries spend 10-100 times more on labor than on software.
- New operating systems will gain a crucial competitive advantage by recording end-to-end work data, which will be unavailable to current incumbents, robotic startups, and frontier AI models.
