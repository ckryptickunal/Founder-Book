## Summary

Wafer is a high-performance AI inference cloud startup founded by University of Chicago graduates that recently raised a $40 million Series A. The company differentiates itself by using AI agents to optimize GPU performance, enabling significantly faster inference speeds for low-latency and high-throughput applications like voice agents and coding assistants.

## Key Ideas

- AI agents can be used to optimize GPU performance and kernels, leading to 2x-3x performance gains over standard inference providers.
- Speed is a critical moat in the AI market, directly impacting user retention and behavior in real-time applications.
- The shift from selling optimization services to providing a direct inference cloud allowed Wafer to scale from zero to $8 million ARR in four months.
- Open-source models are becoming increasingly competitive with proprietary models, especially when paired with specialized inference optimization.
- Exceptional performance engineering is often a result of solving complex, real-world technical problems rather than following rigid job descriptions.
