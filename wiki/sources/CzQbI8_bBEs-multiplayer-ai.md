# Multiplayer AI

## Metadata

## Summary

The video argues that just as successful work tools like Google Docs and Figma became multiplayer, AI agents must also adopt a collaborative, multiplayer approach. Currently, AI interaction is single-player, but the increasing complexity and duration of AI tasks necessitate shared, live agent sessions where teams can collaborate in real-time. This transformation would turn AI work into a shared, living process applicable across various professional fields, and the video calls for developers to build AI that is multiplayer by default.

## Key Ideas

- Multiplayer functionality has been a key driver of success for modern work tools, replacing solo alternatives.
- AI agents, despite their power, are currently predominantly single-player tools, limiting team collaboration.
- The increasing scale and complexity of tasks performed by AI agents (taking hours, days, or weeks) demand a collaborative, multiplayer approach.
- A vision for multiplayer AI involves live, shared agent sessions where teams can collectively monitor, redirect, and hand off tasks, much like working with a human team member.
- Multiplayer AI has broad applicability across diverse professional domains, including engineering, sales, support, legal, analysis, and marketing.
- There is a call for developers to build AI solutions that are multiplayer by default to meet this emerging need.
