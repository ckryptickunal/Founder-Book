# Solving the Blank Canvas Problem: Gusto's AI Co-Founder

## Metadata

- Video ID: `xpeRVyFFy_Q`

- Published: 2026-07-08T14:00:19Z

## Summary

Eddie Kim, co-founder of Gusto, discusses their new AI product, Gusto co-founder, designed to automate most business processes for small businesses. He explains how the product moves beyond AI as a 'glorified search engine' to an 'agentic' system, leveraging Gusto's existing data and offering proactive advice. Kim shares the product's origin, inspired by Open Claw and rapid prototyping with Cloud Code, and highlights the unique advantages and challenges of building for small businesses. The interview also covers the innovative, fast-paced development process, the power of chat interfaces, and the future vision for AI in empowering entrepreneurs.

## Key Ideas

- Gusto co-founder is an AI-powered product designed to automate a wide range of small business processes, including payroll, HR, time off, and external tasks like weather alerts and customer communication.
- The product aims to solve the 'blank canvas problem' by offering pre-configured automations and leveraging existing customer data, making agentic AI accessible to less technically sophisticated small business owners.
- The idea for Gusto co-founder was sparked by Eddie Kim's personal experience setting up Open Claw and rapid prototyping using Cloud Code during a missed flight layover.
- Small business owners are highly receptive to AI automation because it frees up their time from recurring administrative tasks, allowing them to focus on business growth and strategy.
- Gusto co-founder's development process was highly agile, involving a small team of five 'AI builders' who completed a full-blown launch in just 10 weeks, largely by eschewing traditional meetings, specs, and documentation in favor of continuous coding and direct feedback.
- Chat interfaces (SMS, Slack) are underestimated as powerful and accessible ways for users, especially small business owners, to interact with AI agents.
- Beyond automation, Gusto co-founder aims to act as a proactive 'co-founder,' identifying opportunities like tax credits (e.g., R&D tax credit) and compliance tasks for businesses.
- AI is making it significantly easier to start and run a business, contributing to a secular trend of increased entrepreneurship by automating back-office complexities.

## Notable Claims

- One in five new businesses started today are Gusto customers. Evidence: Stated by the interviewer at the beginning of the transcript.
- Gusto co-founder can automate most business processes for small businesses. Evidence: Eddie Kim describes its capabilities, including running payroll, approving time off, reminding employees, and even external tasks like emailing customers about weather.
- Most people, including small businesses, still use AI as a glorified search engine. Evidence: Eddie Kim states, 'One of the issues that I see is, especially, uh, for small businesses, is they really still use AI as sort of a glorified search engine.'
- The 'blank canvas problem' hinders broader AI adoption for complex tasks. Evidence: Eddie Kim explains that starting with powerful but open-ended AI capabilities leads to users not knowing what to do, which Gusto co-founder addresses by suggesting automations.
- Chat (SMS, Slack) is a superior interface for interacting with AI compared to web browsers. Evidence: Eddie Kim's personal experience with Open Claw and customer feedback on Gusto co-founder confirm this, noting the accessibility and ease of interaction.
- Gusto co-founder was built by five people in 10 weeks. Evidence: Eddie Kim states, 'This is actually built by five people, five AI builders over the course of 10 weeks. From start to finish.'

- Traditional development processes (meetings, specs, Jira) were largely skipped for Gusto co-founder's initial build. - AI makes it a 'step function easier' to start a business. Evidence: Eddie Kim states, 'Now I think with AI like it's like kind of a step function easier than even before to start a business.'
