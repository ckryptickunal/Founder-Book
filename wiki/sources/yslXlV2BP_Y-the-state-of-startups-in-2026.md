# The State of Startups in 2026

## Summary

The Y Combinator team discusses the 2026 startup landscape, highlighting a massive shift toward 'hard tech' (robotics, defense, energy, and compute infrastructure) and the rise of agentic workflows. They note that AI-driven coding tools are accelerating product development, enabling smaller teams—and even solo founders—to achieve significant revenue milestones much faster than in previous years.

## Key Ideas

- Hard tech companies in YC batches have grown from 8% to 20% of total intake.
- AI agents are transforming software from point solutions into end-to-end automated workflows.
- The median revenue for YC companies during the batch has increased from $8k to $20k MRR.
- Experienced founders (late 30s to 50s) are seeing a resurgence due to their ability to manage AI agents effectively.
- Data and RL environments for AI labs have become a massive, high-revenue category for startups.
- The barrier to entry for solo founders has lowered significantly due to AI-assisted coding and product development.
