# Proving You’re Human

## Metadata

- Video ID: `nB7QBdjrOwE`
- Channel: Y Combinator
- Published: 2026-07-25T12:12:23Z
- URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nB7QBdjrOwE

## Summary

The internet's foundational trust layer is collapsing due to the rise of cheap, ultra-realistic deepfakes, which are enabling an explosion of online fraud, exemplified by a $25 million deepfake scam. Traditional methods of verifying human identity online are obsolete. The video argues that rebuilding this trust layer, ensuring verified human presence in online interactions without sacrificing privacy, is a critical problem for the next decade. Such a solution would not only prevent scams but also revolutionize social media, dating apps, and online reviews, making its creator a pivotal player in the digital economy.

## Key Ideas

- Deepfake technology (voice clones, fake video calls) is now cheap and ultra-realistic, leading to an explosion of online fraud.
- Traditional trust signals for verifying human identity online are no longer reliable.
- Rebuilding the internet's trust layer to confirm a verified human is on the other end of online interactions is a critical problem for the next decade.
- An ideal solution must balance robust human verification with user privacy.
- The benefits of a reliable human verification system extend beyond fraud prevention to improving social media (no bots), dating apps (real matches), and online reviews (authentic feedback).
- The entity that successfully builds this trust layer will become a foundational component for banks, apps, and video calls across the internet.
