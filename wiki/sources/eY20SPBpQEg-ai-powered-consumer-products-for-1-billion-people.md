## Summary

The transcript argues that AI represents the biggest platform shift since the web and mobile, poised to create new consumer giants. It highlights that AI intelligence is now 'good enough' to treat agents like people and is rapidly becoming 'cheap enough' (costs falling 10x annually), making it the opportune moment for building AI-powered consumer products. The speaker encourages founders to innovate across various aspects of daily life, from productivity to health and social connection, emphasizing that early builders will dominate this emerging market.

## Key Ideas

- Every major platform shift (web, mobile, AI) leads to the creation of new consumer giant companies.
- AI is identified as the most significant platform shift to date.
- Despite being three years into the AI shift, ChatGPT is noted as the only new consumer icon on home screens.
- The timing is ideal for building AI-powered consumer products because AI intelligence has reached a sufficient level of capability ('good enough').
- The cost of running AI (token usage) is rapidly decreasing (10x per year), making it economically viable for mass consumer adoption ('cheap enough').
- The 'consumer moment' for AI is predicted to arrive very soon.
- Early innovators who build AI consumer products now will establish market ownership.
- AI will transform fundamental aspects of daily life, including how people work, travel, learn, stay healthy, manage finances, play, and connect socially.
- Y Combinator is actively seeking founders building AI solutions for a billion people.
