# How Universities Should Prepare Founders

## Metadata

- Video ID: `pg-how-universities-should-prepare-founders`
- Channel: Paul Graham (web)
- Published: 2026-08
- URL: https://www.paulgraham.com/prepare.html

## Summary

Paul Graham’s August 2026 answer to how universities should prepare founders: do not add an entrepreneurship curriculum. Teach students to build things — computer science, mechanical engineering, molecular biology, and other creative disciplines — make starting a startup feel like a real option, and encourage personal projects. YC looks for people who are already good at building and have a habit of doing it.

## Key Ideas

- YC partners look for people who are good at building things and already do it.
- The hard part of startups is product, which comes from building skill, not management or finance classes.
- Entrepreneurship programs, innovation centers, and business-school frames often pull universities off the useful path.
- Startup culture spreads by example: seeing real founders makes the path both desirable and accessible.
- Students should work on their own projects; that habit is what YC later selects for.
