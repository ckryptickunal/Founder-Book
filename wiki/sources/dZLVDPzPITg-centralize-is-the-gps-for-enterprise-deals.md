## Summary

Centralize is an enterprise sales intelligence platform that functions as a 'GPS for deals' by automatically generating dynamic organizational charts and relationship maps. By aggregating unstructured data from emails, calls, and CRMs, the platform helps sales teams identify key stakeholders, visualize institutional memory, and navigate complex enterprise accounts to accelerate sales cycles.

## Key Ideas

- Centralize creates a 'trust graph' by parsing unstructured data from communication channels to map human dynamics.
- The platform solves the problem of 'institutional memory' loss when employees leave or change roles within a prospect organization.
- It enables 'multi-threading' in sales by identifying missing stakeholders and providing warm pathways to decision-makers.
- The tool serves as a collaborative 'chessboard' for sales reps and their internal champions to strategize deal progression.
- Entity resolution is a core technical challenge that allows the platform to track individuals across different companies and timeframes.
