## Summary

Puddle is developing a novel and efficient method for recursive self-improvement (RSI) in AI, which they describe as the 'holy grail' where AI systems enhance themselves. Their approach aims to be significantly faster and cheaper than conventional methods, which typically involve expensive and time-consuming retraining of large language models (LLMs). This contrasts with major AI labs like Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google, who are also exploring RSI but often rely on the costly process of training new models for each improvement step.

## Key Ideas

- Puddle is building a recursively self-improving AI system.
- Recursive self-improvement (RSI) is considered the 'holy grail' of AI, enabling AI to make itself smarter.
- Puddle's method for RSI is claimed to be far faster and cheaper than existing proposals.
- Traditional RSI approaches often require training new Large Language Models (LLMs) from scratch, costing hundreds of millions of dollars and months of effort.
- Major AI companies like Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google are also exploring RSI, but typically use the expensive model retraining approach for each improvement step.
