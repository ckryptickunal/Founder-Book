## Summary

Arendel, a defense startup founded by military veterans, provides comprehensive drone defense ecosystems including hardware, software, and training. The company focuses on autonomous terminal homing interceptor drones to counter threats like Shahed drones, with plans to expand into missile defense systems.

## Key Ideas

- Development of autonomous terminal homing interceptor drones to simplify defense operations.
- Providing end-to-end defense ecosystems including training and hardware for countries lacking effective drone defense.
- Scaling from quadcopter-based drone defense to jet-powered interceptors and eventually missile defense systems.
- The goal of achieving 'affordable mass' in airspace defense to ensure high interception success rates.
