## Summary

Financial compliance is currently inefficient, expensive, and fragmented, relying on manual processes, siloed tools, and significant human capital. As businesses expand, compliance complexity and costs grow disproportionately. The video argues this is an "AI-native problem," as tasks like monitoring regulatory changes, flagging anomalies, and generating reports can be handled faster and cheaper by AI. Most existing solutions are outdated, built around manual workflows. Y Combinator is seeking founders to build AI-native compliance infrastructure that consolidates tools, reduces reliance on specialized headcount, and provides real-time visibility, ultimately rethinking compliance operations with AI as the default.

## Key Ideas

- Current financial compliance processes are inefficient, expensive, and fragmented, relying on manual work and siloed tools.
- The complexity and cost of compliance escalate rapidly as businesses expand into new markets.
- Many core compliance tasks, such as monitoring, reporting, and anomaly detection, are well-suited for AI automation, making it an "AI-native problem."
- Existing compliance solutions are largely outdated, built around manual workflows and human review bottlenecks.
- There is a significant opportunity for founders to build AI-native compliance infrastructure that consolidates tools, reduces specialized headcount, and offers real-time regulatory visibility.
- The ideal AI-native compliance solution should not just automate existing processes but fundamentally rethink compliance operations with AI as the default.
