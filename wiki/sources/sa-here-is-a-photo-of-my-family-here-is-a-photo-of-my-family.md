## Summary

On 10 April 2026, after a Molotov cocktail was thrown at his house, Sam Altman posted a family photo and a personal note. He argues that images and narratives have power, restates his beliefs about abundant and democratized AI, admits being conflict-averse and mishandling the prior OpenAI board fight, and asks the industry to de-escalate rhetoric so there are fewer explosions in fewer homes.

## Key Ideas

- Altman shared a family photo hoping it would deter a repeat attack.
- He treats fear of AI as justified and says safety is a society-wide problem, not just model alignment.
- He does not want a few labs making the most consequential decisions about the future.
- He is not proud of being conflict-averse or of how he handled the previous OpenAI board conflict.
- He frames AGI as having a “ring of power” dynamic and wants no one to hold the ring.
