## Summary

Olivier Pomel, co-founder and CEO of Datadog, discusses the company's journey from a rejected YC applicant in 2010 to a leading cloud-era observability platform. He emphasizes the importance of a culture built on resilience, the value of long-term co-founder relationships, and a product-led growth strategy that balances customer feedback with internal innovation.

## Key Ideas

- Early rejection and hardship can forge a stronger, more disciplined company culture.
- Maintaining a strong, communicative relationship between co-founders is critical for navigating high-stakes decisions like acquisition offers.
- Leadership must actively 'look down' at the ground-level reality of the business rather than relying on filtered, top-down narratives.
- Product strategy should balance incremental customer feedback with strategic top-down initiatives and bottom-up internal innovation.
- AI is shifting the value in software development from writing code to validation and operations, creating new opportunities for observability platforms.
- Conservative hiring growth prevents the organizational dysfunction often caused by rapid, unsustainable scaling.
