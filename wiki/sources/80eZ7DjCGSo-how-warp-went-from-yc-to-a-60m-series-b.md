# How Warp Went From YC to a $60M Series B

## Metadata

- Video ID: `80eZ7DjCGSo`

## Summary

Ayush, founder and CEO of Warp, an AI-native employee management platform, discusses his journey from a small Indian town to founding a successful startup. Warp, which automates complex payroll and compliance for over 1,000 high-growth companies, recently secured a $60 million Series B round led by Battery Ventures. Ayush explains Warp's 'AI-native' approach, contrasting its lean operational model with traditional HR tech incumbents. He delves into the strategic advantages of AI-native companies, the concept of 'systems of record' versus 'systems of intelligence' in the AI era, and Warp's plans to expand its platform and launch a customer-facing AI agent for natural language workflow automation.

## Key Ideas

- Ayush's unique background and journey from India to MIT, driven by an early interest in physics, leading to a career in machine learning and startups.
- The pivot from consumer social apps to B2B payroll and HR, motivated by personal frustration with complex compliance and the 'unsexy' nature of the problem (Schlep Blindness).
- Warp's initial market 'wedge' was solving multi-state payroll complexity, which was exacerbated by remote work trends and the unique, stringent requirements of payroll taxes.
- Defining 'AI-native': building a company and product architecture where all work, especially deep compliance workflows, can be performed by AI agents, leading to significantly leaner operations compared to legacy HR tech.
- The competitive landscape for AI-native startups against incumbents, emphasizing the difficulty of retrofitting AI onto existing systems and the advantage of greenfield development for technical founders.
- The evolution from 'systems of record' (shared truth databases) to 'systems of intelligence' (platforms where AI agents can natively act on underlying data with guardrails and workflows).
- Warp's Series B funding will be used to expand its comprehensive employee management platform (benefits, device management, global contractors) and build robust AI agent infrastructure.
- The upcoming launch of a customer-facing AI agent that allows users to automate complex HR workflows using natural language, eliminating the need for coding or manual button-clicking.
