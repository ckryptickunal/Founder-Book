## Summary

Castle AI, founded by Nitesh and Rishi, is an AI-driven platform that acts as an 'AI employee' to automate back-office operations for banks, specifically focusing on mortgage servicing and consumer lending. After pivoting from real estate and SDR tools during their YC Summer 24 batch, the company successfully scaled to process $2 billion in transactions by solving high-volume, regulated operational tasks that previously required human agents.

## Key Ideas

- Banks struggle with 'change management' and legacy systems, making it difficult to adopt new technology without massive, multi-year infrastructure overhauls.
- Castle AI functions as an 'AI employee' rather than a system of record, allowing banks to deploy automation without replacing core legacy systems.
- The founders pivoted from a 'nice-to-have' product to a 'must-have' solution by identifying a critical pain point: millions of Americans still pay mortgages via phone calls handled by human agents.
- Operating in highly regulated industries requires building deterministic, compliant 'hardness' around non-deterministic LLMs.
- The company successfully used a 'speed-run' strategy, including moving into a client's office in Phoenix, to ensure their product went live.
